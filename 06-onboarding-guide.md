# Funarchy Cooperator Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Funarchy! This guide walks you through your first month.

---

## Week 1: Orientation

### Day 1: Meet the Team

**When:** 1-hour video call  
**Who:** You, jkbo, + other cooperators

**Agenda:**
- Introductions (who you are, what you care about, what you're excited about)
- Overview of Funarchy (values, goals, current state)
- Your role and what success looks like for you
- Questions (ask everything)

### Day 2–3: Self-Guided Tour

Read (in order):
1. `01-charter.md` - Our values
2. `03-roadmap.md` - Our 3-phase plan
3. `02-bylaws.md` - Legal structure
4. `04-amendment-process.md` - How we change things
5. `products/README.md` - The products and the manifesto

**Do this:** Take notes on what's unclear, what excites you, what worries you.

### Day 4: Culture Call

**When:** 1-hour video call  
**Who:** You + a familiar cooperator (not jkbo)

**Goal:** Make sure our values aren't just words; they're lived.

**Discuss:**
- What concerns you about the five principles?
- How we handle disagreement
- What "non-exploitation" means in practice
- How decisions actually get made (theory vs. practice)
- What to do if something feels off

### Day 5: Technical Setup

**Tasks:**
- [ ] GitHub access: added to organization
- [ ] Slack/Discord: in main + domain channels
- [ ] Password manager: shared credentials
- [ ] Crypto wallet: set up for first paycheck (jkbo walks you through)
- [ ] Calendar: recurring meetings (weekly domain sync, bi-weekly all-hands, monthly 1-on-1)

**If anything doesn't work, ask. We fix it.**

---

## Week 2–3: Learning Your Domain

### Infrastructure Team

**First project:** Deploy and maintain a Space instance
- Read: the Space README
- Task: Deploy a test Space instance on Hetzner (with guidance)
- Goal: Understand infrastructure; be ready for customer deployments

### Education Team

**First project:** Review existing curriculum
- Task: Propose 1–2 improvements or additions (small, doable in a day)
- Goal: Understand pedagogy; contribute to content direction

### Governance Team

**First project:** Facilitate one discussion
- Task: Run a GitHub discussion on a governance topic (TBD with jkbo)
- Goal: Get comfortable with community engagement

## Month 1: Settling In

### Weekly Domain Syncs (30–60 min)

**Agenda:**
- What did you do this week?
- What are you stuck on?
- What's next?

### Bi-Weekly All-Hands (60 min)

**Who:** Whole organization  
**Agenda:**
- Announcements, updates, celebrations
- Cross-domain issues (decisions needed)
- Financial update (treasury report)
- Open discussion

### 1-on-1 with Domain Lead (30 min, monthly)

**Agenda:**
- How are you doing? (honestly, not just work)
- Are you aligned with the values?
- Are you paid fairly, treated well?
- Anything you want to change?

## First Paycheck

**Timeline:**
1. By day 5 of month: Tell jkbo your hours/deliverables
2. By day 25: jkbo prepares payment
3. By day 28: Multi-sig transaction signed (requires 2 people)
4. Within 1 day: Payment in your wallet (on-chain, instant)
5. You cash out: Send USDC to Kraken/Coinbase, convert to fiat (~2% fee)

**Total time:** 1–2 days from transaction to bank account

**Example:** You earn $2,000
- Funarchy wallet: −$2,000 USDC
- Your wallet: +$2,000 USDC (within 1 block, ~1 min)
- You send to Kraken → converts → sends to your bank (~$1,960 after 2% fee)

## How to Get Unblocked

**Technical question?**
- First: Ask in domain Slack channel
- Second: Open a GitHub issue
- Third: Grab jkbo/relevant person for a quick sync

**Governance question?**
- First: Read `decisions.md` (decision log)
- Second: Ask in Slack #governance
- Third: Bring it up in all-hands or monthly 1-on-1

**Concern (I disagree, feel exploited, burnt out)?**
- Immediately: Tell domain lead or jkbo (1-on-1, private)
- Option: Request external mediation
- Outcome: We fix it or change the situation
